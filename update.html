Updating Notturno

A new version takes a couple of minutes to put in. Your settings, and where the widget sits on your desktop, stay as they are.

  1. Download the new version

    It arrives in your Downloads folder as Notturno.dmg.

    Download Notturno

  2. Quit the Notturno you have now

    Click the crescent moon in the menu bar, near the clock, and choose Quit Notturno. The old one has to be closed before the new one can take its place.

  3. Open the download and drag Notturno onto Applications

    Double-click Notturno.dmg. A small window opens with two things in it: drag Notturno onto the Applications folder beside it.

  4. Choose Replace

    Your Mac notices there is already a Notturno in Applications and asks what to do. Click Replace.

  5. Open Notturno again

    Open your Applications folder and double-click Notturno. Your Mac will stop it, as it did the first time you installed it, with a box saying Apple could not verify “Notturno”. Click Done (not Move to Trash).

  6. Tell your Mac to open it anyway

    Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and scroll down to the Security section, where it says “Notturno” was blocked. Click Open Anyway, enter your Mac’s password if asked, and click Open Anyway once more.

    The widget comes back where you left it, and the crescent moon returns to the menu bar. This happens once for each new version. You can now eject the download and throw Notturno.dmg away.

    On macOS 13 or 14 there is a shorter way: in Applications, hold Control and click Notturno, choose Open, then Open again.
